Audioreactive Visualization

I related sounds of spoken langauges, words and letters. So I created a audioreative sketch which uses the microphone input to visualize the amplitude of spoken words and letters. When the volume goes up, the letters will be bigger. I had promblems with loading the p5 sound library, so I asked our LLM to help me load the p5 sound library.
