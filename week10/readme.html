Gestrual Control

For this week's assignment, I explored the use of ml5.js to create a sketch that responds to hand gestures. I used the Handpose model to detect hand positions and movements, I used the webcam to track the positions of my fingertips, and calculated the distance between them to control the behavior of the particle ball. Qhen my fingertips are detected to be close to each other, the little particle ball will squeeze to a sphere. When my fingertips are detected to be far away from each other, the little particle ball will scatter. I didn't show the control outlines in the sketch, because I think this piece gives a mystery feeling, and I want the audience to explore the interaction by themselves. Through this assignment, I learned how to use ml5.js to make the coding art more tangible and interactive.
