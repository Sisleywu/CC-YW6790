Stars

I wanted to create a four point star shape, so I tried with regular shapes at the beginning, and then I asked our LLM about how to create stars, then I use the map function and our nested loop to make the inner and outer radius different, and also adding a vertex to it. I also played witht the mousePressed function, to change it's color. Then I used functions from the library to create beautiful pattern. However, my star isn't in the p5 Polar library, so I just change the numbers and rotations for my star with a its own function. I played with different polar shapes.
